The Red Library sound-effect collection is published on the Internet Archive under
[CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/):
<https://archive.org/details/Red_Library_Crowds_Applause> and sibling items.

Each clip was cut to its loudest window, normalised to about −1 dBFS, faded at both edges
and encoded to 64 kbps mono AAC. The whole folder is roughly 220 KB, and files are fetched
lazily the first time a sound plays, so they cost nothing on first paint.

Every other sound in the app is synthesized in the browser by `public/js/core/memes.js`
and ships no audio at all.
